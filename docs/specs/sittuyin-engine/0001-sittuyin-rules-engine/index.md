# 0001. Implement pure TypeScript Sittuyin rules engine

**Date**: 2026-11-22
**Status**: Accepted

## Summary

You are building the pure, zero dependency Sittuyin (traditional Burmese chess) rules engine inside `packages/sittuyin-engine` so both the browser board (`apps/web`) and the authoritative Colyseus server (`apps/server`) enforce the exact same rules. The engine covers the staggered starting pawn wall, dynamic officer setup behind the pawns (supporting hidden simultaneous curtain setup by default plus alternating visible drops for bots and replays), all six traditional piece movements, General (`စစ်ကဲ`) promotion on `sit ke myin` diagonals, illegal stalemate filtering, tournament draw conditions, and Fairy Stockfish compatible FEN and UCI notation. Every public call is a pure immutable function returning a typed `Result` object (a safe success or error union that never crashes rooms on untrusted input), making rules testing, hidden setup privacy, and replay scrubbing straightforward.

## Requirements

**User stories**:

- As a Sittuyin player, I want to arrange my 8 officers secretly behind my pawn line (or pick a classic Myanmar preset and tweak it) so every match opens with authentic dynamic strategy.
- As a player in an endgame, I want accurate traditional General (`စစ်ကဲ`) promotion, illegal stalemate protection, and fair draw rules so matches finish cleanly without deadlocks or disputes.
- As a developer building `apps/web` and `apps/server`, I want one pure, immutable TypeScript rules engine with typed error results, hidden setup view masking, and Fairy Stockfish compatible FEN and UCI helpers so client previews, server validation, replays, and future bots stay 100% in sync.

**Acceptance criteria**:

- **AC-1**: Starting game state places the 16 traditional staggered pawns (`a3` to `d3` and `e4` to `h4` for Red; `a5` to `d5` and `e6` to `h6` for Black) on an 8 by 8 board with 8 officers in hand per side (`1 king`, `1 general`, `2 rook`, `2 elephant`, `2 knight`), keeps zero runtime dependencies, and preserves backwards compatible exports (`BOARD_SIZE`, `Side`, `PieceRole`, `PieceNameEntry`, `SITTUYIN_PIECE_NAMES`, `ENGINE_METADATA`).
- **AC-2**: Hidden simultaneous setup (`setupMode: "hidden_simultaneous"`, default) supports placing from hand, moving or swapping placed officers, removing back to hand, applying a preset, clearing all, locking in when all 8 officers are placed, and unlocking before the opponent also locks in; enforces Rooks strictly on rank 1 (Red) or rank 8 (Black) and the other 6 officers strictly behind own pawns; and merges both drafts atomically onto `board`, records `initialSetupFen`, and transitions to `"playing"` with Red to move once both lock in.
- **AC-3**: Alternating visible drops (`setupMode: "alternating_drops"`) lets Red and Black alternate dropping 1 officer per turn obeying the exact same placement zones until all 16 officers are placed, transitions automatically to `"playing"` with Red to move, and rejects board moves while any officer remains unplaced.
- **AC-4**: Built in traditional Myanmar opening presets (`OPENING_PRESETS` with Myanmar, romanized, and English labels and 180 degree mirrored Black placements), deterministic `generateRandomSetup(seed)`, and deterministic `autoCompleteSetup(state, side, seed)` all produce legal 8 officer setups, with `autoCompleteSetup` preserving existing draft officers, placing unplaced Rooks on the back rank first before remaining officers, and automatically locking in that side (committing both drafts to `"playing"` if the opponent is already locked).
- **AC-5**: All six piece roles generate exact traditional Sittuyin moves (King 1 step any direction with no castling, General 1 step diagonally, Chariot orthogonal slides, Elephant 4 diagonals plus 1 straight forward, Knight L jumps, Pawn 1 step straight forward push and 1 step diagonal forward capture with no double step and no en passant) and reject any move leaving the moving side's King in check.
- **AC-6**: General (`စစ်ကဲ`) promotion is legal only when the moving side has 0 Generals on the board, is always optional, takes a separate turn either in place (`from === to`) or stepping 1 square diagonally onto an empty square (never capturing, check and discovered check allowed when `allowPromotionCheck` is true), applies to pawns standing on enemy half `sit ke myin` squares (`a8, b7, c6, d5, e5, f6, g7, h8` for Red; `a1, b2, c3, d4, e4, f3, g2, h1` for Black), and allows the last surviving pawn to promote from any square when `allowLastPawnPromoteAnywhere` is true.
- **AC-7**: Check and checkmate detection identifies attacks from all six piece types (including checks delivered by promotions), returns checking squares via `getCheckingSquares`, and ends the match as `{ status: "win", winner, reason: "checkmate" }` with highest terminal priority when the side to move is in check with 0 base legal replies (King safe and satisfying `allowPromotionCheck`).
- **AC-8**: Illegal stalemate enforcement (default `stalemateRule: "illegal"`) removes any candidate move that leaves the opponent not in check with 0 base legal replies (using a strict 1 ply check so lookahead never recurses infinitely), ends the match as `{ status: "draw", winner: null, reason: "unavoidable_stalemate" }` when a non checked side has at least 1 base legal move yet 0 legal non stalemating moves, and supports optional `stalemateRule: "draw"` for custom rooms.
- **AC-9**: Automatic draw detection ends the match cleanly on threefold repetition (`positionCounts[key] >= 3`, resolving perpetual check), bare King dead position insufficient material (`K vs K`, `K+N vs K`, `K+F vs K`, `K+P vs K`), standard 50 move rule (`halfmoveClock >= 100` without capture, pawn advance, or promotion), and bare King 50 move countdown (`bareKingClock >= 100` once either side has only its King, resetting only on capture), always yielding to checkmate when both trigger on the same move.
- **AC-10**: Extended Sittuyin FEN (`toFen`, `fromFen`) interoperates with Fairy Stockfish and PyChess (`K F R S N P` for Red, `k f r s n p` for Black, `[hand]` bracketed pocket when non empty, `w` for Red and `b` for Black, halfmove and fullmove counters), always emits a valid FEN (including the full uncommitted pool during hidden setup), round trips losslessly, detects terminal states on import, and strictly rejects malformed syntax, missing or duplicate Kings, more than 1 General per side, impossible piece totals, invalid drop hand parity, or the non moving side already in check.
- **AC-11**: Sittuyin UCI (`toUci`, `parseUci`, `applyUci`) encodes and parses normal moves (`e4e5`), officer drops (`R@a1`), in place promotions (`c6c6f`), and diagonal promotions (`c6b5f`); `replayMatch(initialSetupFen, uciMoves, rules?)` validates and expands saved matches into `uciMoves.length + 1` per ply snapshots (`[initialState, ...afterEachPly]`) for both `hidden_simultaneous` (revealed 32 piece `initialSetupFen` plus play moves) and `alternating_drops` (pawn plus hand `initialSetupFen` plus drop and play moves), returning a structured error with the 1 indexed failing ply on invalid input.
- **AC-12**: Bilingual move display formatter (`formatMoveDisplay(record, locale)`) returns clean English (`"en"`) and Myanmar (`"my"`) move strings covering drops, quiet moves, captures, in place and diagonal promotions, checks, and checkmate.
- **AC-13**: Public API functions are pure, immutable, and deterministic (zero `Math.random()` or `Date.now()`), return typed `Result<T, EngineError>` unions with stable error codes on every fallible call without throwing, and export full UI query helpers (`getLegalMovesFrom`, `getLegalSetupSquares`, `isInCheck`, `getCheckingSquares`, `getPromotionCandidates`, `isSitKeMyinSquare`, `SIT_KE_MYIN_SQUARES`, `INITIAL_PAWN_SQUARES`).
- **AC-14**: `toPlayerView(state, viewer)` enforces hidden setup privacy by stripping opponent draft squares and masking opponent `hands` back to `INITIAL_OFFICER_HAND` for `"red"` and `"black"` viewers (exposing only `setupReady` and `placedCounts` from `0` to `8`) and hiding both drafts and hands for `"spectator"` viewers until both sides lock in.

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Option 1: Pure zero dependency immutable functional TypeScript engine with dual setup modes and Fairy Stockfish compatible FEN and UCI notation.

Build `packages/sittuyin-engine` as a pure, immutable, functional TypeScript library backed by a flat 64 element internal board, typed `Result<T, EngineError>` validation, dual setup modes (`hidden_simultaneous` default plus `alternating_drops`), curated Myanmar presets, strict traditional promotion and illegal stalemate rules, and Fairy Stockfish compatible FEN and UCI serialization.

### Sittuyin rules specification

**1. Board coordinates and starting pawn layout**:

- Files `a` to `h` map to `fileIndex` `0` to `7`; ranks `1` to `8` map to `rankIndex` `0` to `7`; flat index is `rankIndex * 8 + fileIndex` (`a1 = 0`, `h8 = 63`).
- `rotateSquare180(file, rank) = (7 - file, 7 - rank)` (`index' = 63 - index`).
- Red pawns start on `a3, b3, c3, d3, e4, f4, g4, h4` (advancing toward rank 8, `dr = +1`).
- Black pawns start on `a5, b5, c5, d5, e6, f6, g6, h6` (advancing toward rank 1, `dr = -1`).
- Starting officer hand per side: `1 king`, `1 general`, `2 rook`, `2 elephant`, `2 knight` (8 officers per side; 32 pieces total).

**2. Officer setup zones and modes**:

- **Rook setup zone**: Red `a1` to `h1` (rank 1 only, 8 squares); Black `a8` to `h8` (rank 8 only, 8 squares).
- **Non rook officer setup zone** (`king`, `general`, `elephant`, `knight`):
  - Red (20 squares): `a1` to `h1`, `a2` to `h2`, and `e3, f3, g3, h3` (strictly behind Red's starting pawns).
  - Black (20 squares): `a8` to `h8`, `a7` to `h7`, and `a6, b6, c6, d6` (strictly behind Black's starting pawns).
- Because all 6 non rook officers can occupy at most 6 of the 8 back rank squares, at least 2 back rank squares always remain free for the 2 Rooks in every valid partial draft.
- Because all officers sit strictly behind the pawn walls, no officer can give check on the opening turn after setup finishes.
- **Hidden simultaneous mode (`"hidden_simultaneous"`, default)**: both sides edit `setupDrafts[side]` independently in any order. On `place(role, to)`, if `to` is already occupied in `setupDrafts[side]`, return `SQUARE_OCCUPIED` (use `move` to swap). On `move(from, to)`, if `from === to` return `ok(state)` as a no op; if `from` has no drafted officer return `SQUARE_EMPTY`; if `to` already holds another friendly drafted officer, swap them if and only if both roles are legal on their swapped destination squares (checking `ROOK_MUST_BE_BACK_RANK` before `OUTSIDE_SETUP_ZONE`). On `remove(from)`, return `SQUARE_EMPTY` if empty. On `apply_preset(presetId)`, replace any existing draft completely and zero `hands[side]`. `lock` requires all 8 officers placed (`remainingHandTotal === 0`). `unlock` returns `ok(state)` as a no op if already unlocked, or reverts `setupReady[side]` to `false` while the opponent is not yet locked in. `autoCompleteSetup(state, side, seed)` keeps already placed officers, fills remaining unplaced officers legally (Rooks on back rank first), and automatically calls `lock` (locking immediately if 8 are already placed). When the second side locks in, both drafts commit atomically onto `board`, `setupDrafts` clear, `initialSetupFen` is set to `toFen(committedState)`, `positionCounts[initialPositionKey]` is set to `1`, `fullmoveNumber` is `1`, `turn` is `"red"`, and `phase` becomes `"playing"`.
- **Alternating visible drops (`"alternating_drops"`)**: `initialSetupFen` starts at `"8/8/4pppp/pppp4/4PPPP/PPPP4/8/8[KFRRSSNNkfrrssnn] w - - 0 1"`. Red and Black alternate `applyMove({ kind: "drop", role, to })` for 16 plies (each recorded in `moveHistory` with UCI like `R@a1`, incrementing `fullmoveNumber` after each Black drop to match Fairy Stockfish 1 to 1, reaching `9` when `"playing"` begins). Board moves and promotions are rejected with `INVALID_PHASE` until both hands are empty. After Black's 8th drop, `positionCounts[initialPositionKey]` is set to `1`, `turn` is `"red"`, and `phase` becomes `"playing"`.

**3. Curated traditional Myanmar opening presets (`OPENING_PRESETS`)**:

All five presets place both Rooks on rank 1 and the remaining 6 officers inside Red's legal behind pawn zone (`a1..h1, a2..h2, e3..h3`). Black's placements are derived automatically via `rotateSquare180`.

| Preset ID    | Myanmar / Romanized / English                | Red placements (8 officers)                                                         |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `myin_cha`   | `မြင်းချ` / `Myin cha` / `Knight Vanguard`    | `rook: a1, e1` · `king: b1` · `general: d2` · `elephant: c2, g2` · `knight: e3, f3` |
| `sin_cha`    | `ဆင်ချ` / `Sin cha` / `Elephant Wall`         | `rook: a1, f1` · `king: c1` · `general: e2` · `elephant: d2, f3` · `knight: b2, g2` |
| `yahhta_cha` | `ရထားချ` / `Yahhta cha` / `Central Chariots` | `rook: d1, e1` · `king: b1` · `general: c1` · `elephant: c2, f2` · `knight: b2, g3` |
| `khan_tat`   | `ခံတပ်` / `Khan tat` / `Royal Fortress`        | `rook: a1, h1` · `king: b1` · `general: c2` · `elephant: b2, f2` · `knight: e2, f3` |
| `sit_ke_cha` | `စစ်ကဲချ` / `Sit ke cha` / `General Ambush`    | `rook: d1, h1` · `king: b1` · `general: e3` · `elephant: d2, f2` · `knight: c2, g2` |

**4. Piece movement vectors** (with `dir = +1` for Red and `dir = -1` for Black):

| PieceRole  | FEN (Red/Black) | Movement and capture rules                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `king`     | `K` / `k`       | 1 step in any of 8 directions (`df, dr in {-1, 0, 1}`, not both `0`); empty or enemy; no castling                                                                                         |
| `general`  | `F` / `f`       | 1 step in any of 4 diagonal directions (`df in {-1, 1}`, `dr in {-1, 1}`); empty or enemy                                                                                                 |
| `rook`     | `R` / `r`       | Slides orthogonally along `(0, 1), (0, -1), (1, 0), (-1, 0)` until blocked by friendly piece, board edge, or capturing first enemy piece                                                  |
| `elephant` | `S` / `s`       | 1 step in any of 4 diagonal directions OR 1 step straight forward `(0, dir)` (5 target squares total); empty or enemy                                                                     |
| `knight`   | `N` / `n`       | L jump `({-2, 2} x {-1, 1})` and `({-1, 1} x {-2, 2})`; leaps over pieces; empty or enemy                                                                                                 |
| `pawn`     | `P` / `p`       | Quiet push 1 step straight forward `(0, dir)` onto empty square only; capture 1 step diagonally forward `(-1, dir)` or `(1, dir)` onto enemy square only; no 2 square push; no en passant |

**5. General (`စစ်ကဲ`) promotion rules**:

- **Promotion diagonal squares (`SIT_KE_MYIN_SQUARES`)**:
  - Red (8 squares on enemy half): `a8, b7, c6, d5, e5, f6, g7, h8`.
  - Black (8 squares on enemy half): `a1, b2, c3, d4, e4, f3, g2, h1`.
- **Eligibility**: a friendly pawn on square `from` may generate promotion moves if and only if:
  1. The moving side currently has `0` Generals (`general`) on the board (strict single General limit).
  2. Either `isSitKeMyinSquare(from, side)` is `true`, OR (`rules.allowLastPawnPromoteAnywhere` is `true` AND the moving side has exactly `1` pawn left on the board).
- **Generated promotion moves** (always optional, separate turn):
  - **In place**: `{ kind: "promote", from, to: from, promoteTo: "general" }`.
  - **Diagonal step**: for each of the 4 diagonal offsets `(-1,-1), (1,-1), (-1,1), (1,1)`, if `to` is on board AND `board[to] === null` (strictly empty, never capturing), `{ kind: "promote", from, to, promoteTo: "general" }`.
- **Check and King safety**: every promotion move must leave the moving side's own King not in check (so in place promotion is automatically illegal when already in check, while a diagonal step onto a blocking square is legal if it resolves the check). When `rules.allowPromotionCheck` is `true` (default), promotions that give or discover check are legal; when `false`, they are filtered out.
- **Passed pawn behavior**: if a player has 2 or more pawns and moves a pawn past its `sit ke myin` square without promoting, that pawn cannot promote unless a later diagonal capture lands it on another `sit ke myin` square or all other friendly pawns are captured so it becomes the last surviving pawn.

**6. Check, checkmate, illegal stalemate, and draw priority**:

- **Base legal move (`baseLegalMoves`)**: a pseudo legal move (`normal` or `promote`) that leaves the moving side's own King not attacked by any enemy piece after the move AND satisfies `rules.allowPromotionCheck` (so if `allowPromotionCheck === false`, checking or discovered check promotions are already excluded before checkmate, stalemate, and 1 ply lookahead run, preventing any soft lock).
- **1 ply illegal stalemate filter** (active when `rules.stalemateRule === "illegal"`): for each `baseLegalMoves` candidate `M` by `side`, apply `M` to produce candidate board `B1` and check opponent `opp`:
  - If `opp` IS in check on `B1`: `M` is legal (check or checkmate).
  - If `opp` is NOT in check on `B1`: check whether `opp` has at least 1 **`baseLegalMoves`** reply (including any legal promotion move). If `opp` has `0` base legal replies, `M` stalemates `opp` and is filtered out as illegal (`ILLEGAL_STALEMATE` if attempted directly). Do NOT recurse into whether `opp`'s replies would stalemate `side`.
- **Terminal evaluation priority** (evaluated in exact order after every `"playing"` move is applied and `turn` switches to `nextSide`):
  1. **Checkmate**: `isInCheck(nextState, nextSide)` AND `baseLegalMoves(nextState, nextSide).length === 0` produces `{ status: "win", winner: mover, reason: "checkmate" }`.
  2. **Stalemate / unavoidable stalemate**: `!isInCheck(nextState, nextSide)`:
     - If `baseLegalMoves.length === 0`: produces `reason: "stalemate"` when `stalemateRule === "draw"`, or `reason: "unavoidable_stalemate"` when `stalemateRule === "illegal"` (possible on imported FEN).
     - Else if `stalemateRule === "illegal"` AND `legalMoves.length === 0` (every base legal move would stalemate the opponent): produces `{ status: "draw", winner: null, reason: "unavoidable_stalemate" }`.
  3. **Insufficient material (bare King dead positions)**: `0` rooks and `0` elephants on the board AND total pieces `<= 3` (exact set: `K vs K`, `K+N vs K`, `K+F vs K`, `K+P vs K`; rare same color `K+F vs K+F` intentionally resolves under the 50 move rule) produces `{ status: "draw", winner: null, reason: "insufficient_material" }`.
  4. **Threefold repetition**: `positionCounts[positionKey] >= 3` (where `positionKey` is `<boardFen>[<handFen>] <w|b>`) produces `{ status: "draw", winner: null, reason: "threefold_repetition" }`.
  5. **Standard 50 move rule**: `halfmoveClock >= 100` produces `{ status: "draw", winner: null, reason: "fifty_move_rule" }`.
  6. **Bare King 50 move rule**: `rules.enableBareKingFiftyMove && bareKingClock !== null && bareKingClock >= 100` produces `{ status: "draw", winner: null, reason: "bare_king_fifty_move" }`.
- **Clock update rules**:
  - `halfmoveClock`: `0` throughout `"setup"`; during `"playing"`, resets to `0` on any capture, pawn advance (`normal` move by a pawn), or `promote`, and increments by `1` otherwise.
  - `bareKingClock`: stays `null` whenever `rules.enableBareKingFiftyMove === false` or during `"setup"` or while both sides have more than `1` piece on the board. When `rules.enableBareKingFiftyMove === true`, on the ply that reduces either side to exactly `1` piece (bare King), initializes to `0` (starting the countdown on the next ply). While already non null, resets to `0` if a capture occurs, otherwise increments by `1` (never resets on pawn moves or promotions).
  - `fullmoveNumber`: starts at `1`. In `"hidden_simultaneous"`, stays `1` until play starts, then increments by `1` after each Black ply. In `"alternating_drops"`, increments by `1` after every Black turn across both setup drops and play (matching Fairy Stockfish 1 to 1, reaching `9` after 16 drops).

**7. Notation grammars (FEN, UCI, and bilingual display)**:

- **Sittuyin FEN**: `<ranks>[<hand>] <side> - - <halfmove> <fullmove>`
  - Piece letters: Red uppercase `K` (king), `F` (general), `R` (rook), `S` (elephant), `N` (knight), `P` (pawn); Black lowercase `k, f, r, s, n, p`.
  - Hand pocket `[<hand>]`: `toFen` emits canonical letter order `K F R S N k f r s n` and omits `[]` when both hands are empty; during `"hidden_simultaneous"` setup, `toFen` emits `board` plus the full uncommitted pool (`hands + setupDrafts`, `[KFRRSSNNkfrrssnn]`) so the FEN stays valid and leaks zero draft squares. `fromFen` accepts arbitrary letter order inside `[...]` (for Fairy Stockfish interop), accepts omitted or empty `[]`, and rejects `P`/`p` inside `[...]`.
  - Side to move: `w` (Red) or `b` (Black). All 6 space separated fields are required, with exact `- -` for castling and en passant, non negative integer `halfmove`, and positive integer `fullmove`.
  - Strict `fromFen` validation: allows pawns on any rank `1` to `8` (supporting optional promotion and puzzles). When hands are non empty, forces `rules.setupMode = "alternating_drops"`, counts Kings and piece limits across `board + hands`, enforces alternating drop hand parity (`redHand === blackHand >= 1` when `w` to move; `blackHand === redHand + 1` when `b` to move; `<= 8` per side), validates officers on board against setup zones, initializes `positionCounts = {}` and `bareKingClock = null`, and skips bare King terminal checks. When hands are empty, validates exactly 1 King per side on `board`, `<= 1` General per side, piece count bounds, opponent not in check, initializes `captured = { red: [], black: [] }` and `positionCounts = { [currentKey]: 1 }`, rejects negative or non integer `options.bareKingClock` with `INVALID_FEN`, and evaluates all terminal states including `bare_king_fifty_move` when `enableBareKingFiftyMove` is true, either side is bare King, and `bareKingClock >= 100`.
  - Starting pawn plus hand FEN: `8/8/4pppp/pppp4/4PPPP/PPPP4/8/8[KFRRSSNNkfrrssnn] w - - 0 1`.
- **Sittuyin UCI**:
  - Normal move: `<from><to>` (example: `e4e5`).
  - Officer drop: `<UPPER_FEN_LETTER>@<to>` (examples: `R@a1`, `K@e1`, `F@d2`, `S@c2`, `N@b2`; always uppercase letter per Fairy Stockfish UCI drop convention, side inferred from `state.turn`).
  - In place promotion: `<sq><sq>f` (example: `c6c6f`).
  - Diagonal step promotion: `<from><to>f` (example: `c6b5f`).
  - `parseUci` strictness: strictly case sensitive (`R@a1`, `c6b5f`), rejects `P@<sq>`, bad squares, and same square non promotion strings (`e4e4`) as `INVALID_UCI`, deferring board legality (diagonal distance, occupancy, King safety) to `applyMove`.
- **Bilingual display (`formatMoveDisplay(record, locale)`)**:
  - English (`"en"`): uses player friendly piece abbreviations (`K` King, `G` General, `R` Rook, `E` Elephant, `N` Knight; pawns omit the letter) so UI move lists match English piece names while FEN and UCI keep `F` and `S`. Formats drops as `R@a1` or `G@d2`, quiet moves as `Re1e3` or `e4e5`, captures as `Re1xe5` or `e4xd5`, in place promotion as `c6=G`, diagonal promotion as `c6b5=G`, and suffixes `+` for check and `#` for checkmate.
  - Myanmar (`"my"`): uses `SITTUYIN_PIECE_NAMES[role].myanmar` (`မင်းကြီး`, `စစ်ကဲ`, `ရထား`, `ဆင်`, `မြင်း`, `နယ်`), formatting drops as `<piece> <to>` (`ရထား a1`), quiet normal moves as `<piece> <from> <to>` (`ရထား e1 e3`, `နယ် e4 e5`), captures as `<piece> <from>x<to>` (`နယ် e4xd5`), in place promotion as `စစ်ကဲ <sq>` (`စစ်ကဲ c6`), and diagonal promotion as `စစ်ကဲ <from> <to>` (`စစ်ကဲ c6 b5`). Appends a leading space plus `ချုပ်` when `record.gaveCheckmate` is `true`, or a leading space plus `ခွေ` when `record.gaveCheck && !record.gaveCheckmate` (suppressing `ခွေ` on checkmate).

### State transitions

```
[createGame]
   │
   ├── rules.setupMode === "hidden_simultaneous"
   │     phase = "setup"
   │     ├─ applySetupAction(place | move | remove | apply_preset | clear) -> stays "setup"
   │     ├─ applySetupAction(lock) [one side ready] -> stays "setup" (can still unlock)
   │     └─ applySetupAction(lock) [both sides ready] -> atomic commit drafts to board,
   │        set initialSetupFen, record positionKey=1, turn="red" ──► phase = "playing"
   │
   └── rules.setupMode === "alternating_drops"
         phase = "setup" (initialSetupFen = starting pawn + hand FEN)
         ├─ applyMove(drop) [plies 1..15, toggles turn] -> stays "setup"
         └─ applyMove(drop) [ply 16, hands empty] -> record positionKey=1,
            turn="red" ──► phase = "playing"

[phase = "playing"]
   ├─ applyMove(normal | promote) [no terminal outcome] -> toggle turn, update clocks/history -> stays "playing"
   └─ applyMove(normal | promote) [terminal condition triggered] -> set outcome ──► phase = "finished"

[fromFen]
   ├─ hands non empty -> forces rules.setupMode = "alternating_drops", validates hand parity -> phase = "setup"
   ├─ hands empty & non terminal -> phase = "playing"
   └─ hands empty & terminal (checkmate / stalemate / unavoidable_stalemate / insufficient_material / 50 move / bare_king_fifty_move) -> phase = "finished"
```

### Key invariants

- **Piece count bounds**: at every reachable state, each side has exactly `1` King, at most `1` General, at most `2` Rooks, at most `2` Elephants, at most `2` Knights, and at most `8` Pawns across `board`, `hands`, and `setupDrafts`.
- **Single General invariant**: promotion never creates a second General for the same side; `fromFen` rejects any FEN containing `> 1` General per side.
- **Hidden setup isolation**: during `"hidden_simultaneous"` setup, `state.board` holds only the 16 fixed pawns until both sides lock in; `toFen` emits the full uncommitted pool rather than leaking drafts; `toPlayerView` never exposes opponent draft coordinates or unmasked opponent `hands`.
- **Immutability and determinism**: no public function mutates any input `GameState`, array, or record, and calling any function twice with identical inputs returns deeply equal outputs with zero reliance on system clock or global randomness.
- **Terminal priority invariant**: whenever a move simultaneously delivers checkmate and triggers a 50 move, bare King 50 move, or threefold repetition threshold, `checkmate` always wins.
- **Back rank Rook invariant**: in both setup modes, presets, `generateRandomSetup`, and `autoCompleteSetup`, Rooks are placed strictly on rank 1 (Red) or rank 8 (Black); `autoCompleteSetup` always places unplaced Rooks before remaining officers and locks in automatically.

### Security model

- **Trust boundary**: `packages/sittuyin-engine` contains no authentication, network I/O, or regulated data (zero PII, zero secrets). However, `apps/server` passes untrusted WebSocket payloads (setup actions, UCI moves, preset IDs) directly into it.
- **Safe validation of untrusted input**: every fallible entry point validates bounds, phase, side turn, and legality, returning `Result<T, EngineError>` without throwing uncaught exceptions or permitting prototype pollution keys in maps or FEN parsers.
- **Hidden setup anti cheat boundary**: `apps/server` must transmit only `toPlayerView(state, viewer)` to connected clients (never raw `GameState`) during `phase === "setup"`. Both opponent draft squares and opponent hand role counts are masked back to `INITIAL_OFFICER_HAND` until reveal, and spectators (`viewer = "spectator"`) receive empty `myDraft` and masked hands until reveal, preventing stream sniping during ranked matches.

### Configuration required

None. `packages/sittuyin-engine` requires zero environment variables, zero secrets, and zero runtime npm dependencies. Rule variations are passed explicitly via `RulesConfig`.

- **Alternating visible drops**: 16 alternating drops (`R@a1`, ...) succeed across legal zones, reject board moves on ply 8 (`INVALID_PHASE`), increment `fullmoveNumber` to `9` matching Fairy Stockfish, and transition to `"playing"` with Red to move after ply 16, verifies **AC-3**.
- **Presets and deterministic completion**: all 5 `OPENING_PRESETS` validate cleanly for both Red and Black (180 degree symmetry); `generateRandomSetup("match-42")` returns identical squares across repeated calls; `autoCompleteSetup` with 6 non rook officers already placed on rank 1 still places both remaining Rooks cleanly on the last 2 back rank squares and locks in automatically (committing to `"playing"` if opponent is already locked), verifies **AC-4**.
- **Piece movement geometry**: exhaustive move tests for all 6 roles including Elephant forward asymmetry (Red north only, Black south only), Pawn no double step and no en passant, King no castling, and pinned piece filtering, verifies **AC-5**.
- **General promotion edge cases**: separate turn in place (`c6c6f`) and diagonal empty step (`c6b5f`) succeed when General is gone; blocked when own General is alive; diagonal capture promotion rejected; check and discovered check on promotion allowed by default and cleanly excluded from `baseLegalMoves` with zero soft lock when `allowPromotionCheck: false`; in place promotion rejected while already in check while ray blocking diagonal promotion succeeds; pawn past `sit ke myin` cannot promote when 2 pawns remain, yet promotes anywhere when it becomes the last surviving pawn, verifies **AC-6**, **AC-7**, **AC-8**.
- **Checkmate priority**: checkmate delivered on the 100th halfmove (or 3rd repetition) resolves as `win` (`checkmate`) rather than a draw, and `getCheckingSquares` identifies single and double checkers (and returns `[]` during setup before Kings are placed), verifies **AC-7**, **AC-9**.
- **Illegal stalemate and unavoidable stalemate**: a move leaving the opponent not in check with 0 base legal replies is excluded from `getLegalMoves` and rejected with `ILLEGAL_STALEMATE` on `applyMove`; a position where the opponent's only legal reply is a pawn promotion is NOT treated as stalemate; a forced mutual stalemate position finishes cleanly as `unavoidable_stalemate` with zero infinite recursion; `stalemateRule: "draw"` allows the move and draws as `stalemate`, verifies **AC-8**.
- **All four draw rules**: threefold repetition (including perpetual check loop), bare King dead positions (`K vs K`, `K+N vs K`, `K+F vs K`, `K+P vs K`) drawing immediately while `K+S vs K` continues (and can deliver corner mate `a8`/`a6`/`b7`), standard 50 move draw at 100 plies, and bare King 50 move draw at 100 plies without resetting on pawn pushes while resetting on capture (and staying `null` when `enableBareKingFiftyMove: false`), verifies **AC-9**.
- **Strict FEN round trip and rejection**: `toFen` during hidden setup emits the full uncommitted pool (`[KFRRSSNNkfrrssnn]`) with zero draft leak; `toFen` and `fromFen` round trip setup and midgame positions matching Fairy Stockfish strings (including arbitrary hand letter order); `fromFen` forces `alternating_drops` on non empty hands, rejects uneven drop hand parity, malformed strings, missing Kings, 2 Generals for one side, 3 Rooks, invalid `options.bareKingClock`, or opponent already in check, and flags already checkmated or `bare_king_fifty_move` FENs as `"finished"`, verifies **AC-10**.
- **UCI and match replay**: `parseUci`, `toUci`, and `applyUci` handle `e4e5`, `R@a1`, `c6c6f`, and `c6b5f` while rejecting `P@a1`, lowercase drops, and `e4e4`; `replayMatch` returns `N + 1` snapshots for both hidden setup and alternating drop matches and reports the exact 1 indexed failing `ply` when a corrupt UCI move is injected, verifies **AC-11**.
- **Bilingual display formatting**: `formatMoveDisplay` produces exact `"en"` and `"my"` labels (including quiet moves `ရထား e1 e3`, captures `နယ် e4xd5`, promotions, check `ခွေ`, and checkmate `ချုပ်` suppressing `ခွေ`), verifies **AC-12**.
- **Immutability, Result errors, and UI query helpers**: input `GameState` is frozen (`Object.freeze` in tests) with zero mutation on `applyMove` and `applySetupAction`; invalid inputs return `Result` errors without throwing; `getLegalMovesFrom`, `getPromotionCandidates`, and `isSitKeMyinSquare` agree with `getLegalMoves`, verifies **AC-13**.
- **Hidden setup privacy isolation**: `toPlayerView(state, "red")` omits every Black draft square AND masks `hands.black` back to `INITIAL_OFFICER_HAND` while accurately reporting `placedCounts.black` (`8 - remainingHandTotal`) and `setupReady.black`, and `toPlayerView(state, "spectator")` hides both drafts and hands until both sides lock in, verifies **AC-14**.

## Build plan

Ordered using a **Tracer Bullet** approach (assumed default since `AGENTS.md` and `docs/scope/` are not created yet): stand up a thin playable end to end core first (coordinates, board, alternating drops, basic moves, check/checkmate), then thicken with hidden simultaneous setup, presets, promotion edge cases, illegal stalemate, draws, notation, view masking, and replay. No database migration is needed.

1. **Core types, constants, square math, and immutable board foundation**: implement `types.ts` (including `SetupPlacements`, `SetupAction`, `PlayerGameView`, `Result`), `constants.ts`, and `squares.ts`, preserving existing scaffold exports (`BOARD_SIZE`, `Side`, `PieceRole`, `PieceNameEntry`, `SITTUYIN_PIECE_NAMES`, `ENGINE_METADATA`) and `createGame` starting pawn wall plus officer hands, with Vitest unit tests, satisfies **AC-1**, **AC-13**.
2. **Thin playable core (piece moves, attacks, King safety, check, and checkmate)**: implement `moves.ts` (all 6 traditional piece vectors, attack queries, King safety filtering, `getLegalMoves`, `getLegalMovesFrom`, `isInCheck`, `getCheckingSquares`) and core `rules.ts` (`applyMove`, alternating drop transition, checkmate detection), with Vitest tests covering every piece and checkmate, satisfies **AC-3**, **AC-5**, **AC-7**, **AC-13**.
3. **General (`စစ်ကဲ`) promotion rules and edge cases**: extend `moves.ts` and `rules.ts` with `sit ke myin` checks (`isSitKeMyinSquare`, `getPromotionCandidates`), single General limit, optional separate turn in place and empty diagonal step promotions, check rules, and the last surviving pawn exception, with full edge case unit tests, satisfies **AC-6**, **AC-13**.
4. **Illegal stalemate enforcement and unavoidable stalemate**: add the 1 ply illegal stalemate filter in `moves.ts` (`ILLEGAL_STALEMATE` error code), `unavoidable_stalemate` detection, and optional `stalemateRule: "draw"` support in `rules.ts`, with unit tests including promotion escape and mutual stalemate non recursion, satisfies **AC-8**, **AC-13**.
5. **Draw conditions and terminal priority**: implement canonical position keys, `positionCounts` threefold repetition, strict dead position insufficient material check, standard 50 move `halfmoveClock`, bare King `bareKingClock`, and checkmate first terminal priority in `rules.ts`, with unit tests including `K+S vs K` non draw corner mate, satisfies **AC-7**, **AC-9**.
6. **Hidden simultaneous setup, presets, deterministic PRNG, and privacy view**: implement `prng.ts`, `presets.ts` (`OPENING_PRESETS`, `getPresetPlacements`, `generateRandomSetup`, `autoCompleteSetup`), `setup.ts` (`applySetupAction`, `getLegalSetupSquares`, atomic draft commit), and `view.ts` (`toPlayerView`), with unit tests for back rank Rook guarantees and spectator/opponent draft masking, satisfies **AC-2**, **AC-4**, **AC-13**, **AC-14**.
7. **Sittuyin FEN, UCI, bilingual display, and match replay**: implement `fen.ts` (`toFen`, `fromFen` strict validator), `uci.ts` (`toUci`, `parseUci`, `applyUci`), `display.ts` (`formatMoveDisplay` for `"my"` and `"en"`), `replay.ts` (`replayMatch`), and final `index.ts` exports, plus end to end Vitest replay and round trip test suites, satisfies **AC-10**, **AC-11**, **AC-12**, **AC-13**.

## Consequences

**Positive**:

- One zero dependency pure TypeScript package guarantees identical Sittuyin rules in browser move previews (`apps/web`) and authoritative multiplayer validation (`apps/server`).
- Separating `setupDrafts` from `board` during hidden simultaneous setup eliminates accidental state leaks and makes `toPlayerView` trivial for Colyseus `@view()` integration.
- Fairy Stockfish and PyChess compatible FEN and UCI strings unlock future bot opponents, PGN/replay portability, and external engine analysis without writing a translation layer.
- Pure immutable `Result` functions make unit testing every obscure Burmese chess edge case fast, deterministic, and free of uncaught runtime exceptions.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:

- Checking 1 ply illegal stalemate runs an extra opponent King safety check for every quiet candidate move; while fast (sub millisecond) for interactive human play and server validation, a future minimax AI bot will want a separate lower level bitboard search or fast internal make/unmake layer rather than calling immutable `applyMove` millions of times per second.
- Supporting both `hidden_simultaneous` and `alternating_drops` adds a small dual setup state branch compared to an alternating only engine.
- Strict dead position insufficient material (`K+S vs K` allowed to continue) means two casual players in an unwinnable Elephant endgame play until the bare King 50 move draw triggers unless they agree to a draw in the room layer.

**Neutral**:

- Match lifecycle controls (wall clock chess timers, resignation, mutual draw offers, abort, and reconnection grace periods) deliberately remain outside `packages/sittuyin-engine` and belong to the upcoming Colyseus room spec.
- Using human English letters (`G`, `E`) in `formatMoveDisplay("en")` while keeping Fairy Stockfish letters (`F`, `S`) in FEN and UCI separates player friendly UI text from wire/storage notation.
